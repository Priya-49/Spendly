Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only what is necessary to provide the Service:

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data — basic server logs such as page requests and timestamps, used to keep the Service running reliably.

We do not collect payment information, device identifiers, or precise location data.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate and improve Spendly:

  • To authenticate your account and display your expense records.
  • To generate summaries and category breakdowns visible only to you.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve the reliability of the Service.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the Service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We apply standard technical measures — including encrypted connections and access controls — to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be removed from our systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes. We may use a limited number of trusted service providers (such as hosting infrastructure) to operate the Service. These providers are contractually bound to handle your data only as directed by us and in accordance with this policy.

The Service loads fonts from Google Fonts. This request may expose your IP address to Google; please refer to Google's own privacy policy if you have concerns about this.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. You can manage most of this directly within the app. To request full account deletion or a copy of your data, please contact us using the details below.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the way we handle your data, please reach out via the contact information provided within the app. We will respond to all privacy-related inquiries within a reasonable timeframe.
